01 THE DIFFERENCE

Conversation is
the surface.

The meaningful difference appears after the customer sends a message.

A conventional chatbot may answer, classify or route. A WhatsApp orchestration layer maintains the journey across business rules, approved knowledge, internal owners, configured tools and human decisions until the next valid outcome is reached.

CHATBOT / SCOPE

Answer and navigate

Useful for bounded questions, menus, basic qualification and information retrieval.

ORCHESTRATION / SCOPE

Coordinate the journey

Moves context and responsibility across stages instead of ending at the response.

CHATBOT / CONTROL

Conversation rules

Often governed mainly by prompts, menus, intent handling and escalation.

ORCHESTRATION / CONTROL

Business authority

Adds permissions, approval points, exceptions and evidence around real actions.

02 WHEN TO CHOOSE

Use the smallest system that completes the job.

Choose orchestration when a conversation must reliably cross people, knowledge and business systems.

01

Multiple handoffs

The journey depends on several teams, decisions or timed follow-ups.

02

Consequential actions

The workflow changes records, commitments or customer outcomes and needs control.

03

Persistent context

Approved organizational knowledge and journey history must remain available across stages.

03 FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Choose with
clarity.

Is Relayvo a WhatsApp chatbot?

No. Relayvo uses WhatsApp as the customer-facing channel while coordinating knowledge, workflow steps, approvals and configured business actions behind the conversation.

When is a chatbot enough?

A chatbot can suit bounded questions, navigation and simple information retrieval when no cross-system action or consequential decision is required.

Where do people remain involved?

People remain responsible for exceptions, sensitive decisions and approvals defined by the workflow.
