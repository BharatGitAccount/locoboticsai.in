03 FREQUENT QUESTIONS

A bounded first
workflow.

What can a Relayvo onboarding workflow coordinate?

A configured workflow can guide information collection, answer from approved knowledge, route review, request human approval and trigger the next permitted action.

Does Relayvo make every onboarding decision automatically?

No. Sensitive decisions and exceptions can remain with authorized people while Relayvo coordinates the routine journey around them.

Does Relayvo replace existing business systems?

Relayvo is designed to coordinate journeys across configured systems and owners rather than automatically replace the systems that hold authoritative records.
