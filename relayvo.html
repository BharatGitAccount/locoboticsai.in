05 FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Relayvo,
clearly.

What is WhatsApp Business autonomy?

It is the controlled coordination of a business journey that begins on WhatsApp and moves through knowledge, decisions, approvals and configured actions without requiring people to manually manage every routine handoff.

Is Relayvo a bulk-messaging tool?

No. Messaging is one part of Relayvo. Its primary role is to orchestrate end-to-end customer journeys and enterprise workflows.

Where do people stay involved?

Teams remain responsible at approval points, exceptions and consequential decisions. Relayvo is designed to automate coordination while preserving human authority.

Can Relayvo support privacy-sensitive deployments?

Yes. Relayvo's deployment direction includes on-premise and offline-LLM options for organizations with stricter privacy requirements. The final architecture is matched to the selected workflow and environment.
